**Privacy Policy for Math 3 Merge**

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Math 3 Merge ("the game") is published by Nguyễn Tài Khang ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

**1. Information we collect**
We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers for the game.

**2. Information stored on your device**
Your progress (levels completed, stars, coins, boosters, settings such as sound and theme) is saved only on your device. If you delete the game, this data is removed. We cannot see it or recover it.

**3. Advertising**
This version of the game contains no advertising and no ad trackers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

**4. In-app purchases**
The game offers optional purchases (an unlock of the full game, coin packs and bundles). Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We use Apple's purchase confirmation only to give you what you bought and to let you restore it ("Restore purchases").

**5. Third-party software**
The game is made with the Unity engine and uses Unity's In-App Purchasing package to talk to the App Store. These components may process limited technical information (for example, device and purchase transaction information) as described in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

**6. Children**
The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

**7. Changes to this policy**
If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the game means you accept the updated policy.
